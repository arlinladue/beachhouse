The House

A luxury home from home for larger groups and families coming together.

The house was built in 1896 and is designed in the classic Arts and Crafts style, and decorated to a high standard with an airy and beachy feel. Ten double bedrooms, many ensuite with sea views. Three generous reception rooms, a terrace for al fresco dining with pizza oven, and private gardens overlooking Lyme Bay with a large hot tub and a seasonally heated pool.

Two seasons

Summer: the pool, the terrace and two miles of sand.

Winter: the log fire, the hot tub and a table for twenty.

The pool is heated from April to October. Eat on the terrace with the pizza oven and Weber BBQ, then head down to the beach for rock pooling, sea swimming, paddleboarding or kitesurfing. Outdoor hot showers are waiting when you come back up.

  • Heated pool, Apr–Oct
  • Pizza oven and BBQ
  • Table tennis
  • Outdoor hot showers
  • Lifeguarded beach
  • Watersports lessons

Exmouth and your hosts

Like having your own beach at the bottom of the garden.

Exmouth beach is two miles of golden sand, shallow water and rock pools, with a dog-friendly section all year. The town is a 20 minute walk along the seafront; Dartmoor is 40 minutes by car. Owners Steph and Eric Bridge run Edge Watersports, 100 metres along the beach, and can arrange lessons and hire for every level.
